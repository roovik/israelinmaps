Israel has about 9.4 million people. Among the 22 members of the Arab League, Iran and Israel, it ranks 14 of 25 by population. Egypt is the largest, with about twelve times as many people. The League and Iran together have about 60 times Israel’s population.

The 22 Arab League members together have about 477 million people. The West Bank and the Gaza Strip are shown separately. The Arab League counts them together as one member, Palestine.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Population, total, 2024 or 2025 estimate. The West Bank and the Gaza Strip are the separate Factbook entries.
