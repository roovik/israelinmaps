Venture capital raised by startups in 2025, in US dollars per inhabitant. Israel in blue.

Israeli technology companies raised about $16.7 billion in 2025. Divided among about 9.4 million people, that is about $1,776 per person, more than twice the United States figure of $811.

The totals come from different trackers that count deals differently, so the chart is a guide to scale and not a precise ranking. It covers ten countries for which a 2025 total was available.

Sources. 2025 totals as compiled by the Mean CEO blog from Startup Nation Central (Israel), Crunchbase (United States), HSBC Innovation Banking and Dealroom (United Kingdom), Tracxn (India), Startbase (Germany), The French Tech Journal (France), CVCA (Canada), the Korean Ministry of SMEs and Startups, and Wamda (UAE, Saudi Arabia).
