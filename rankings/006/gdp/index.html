GDP per capita at purchasing power parity, in 2021 dollars, 2023 to 2024 estimates. Israel in blue.

Israel’s economy is about 540 billion dollars at official exchange rates. That is about 15 percent of the combined economy of all 22 Arab League members, about 3.7 trillion, with about 2.0 percent of their population. Iran, at 437 billion, is smaller than Israel at exchange rates.

Per person, Israel ranks 5 of 24 on GDP per capita at purchasing power parity, behind Qatar, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Bahrain, and ahead of Kuwait and Oman.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive.
