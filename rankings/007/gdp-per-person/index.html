Israel ranks 37 of 196 countries and economies in GDP per person at purchasing power parity, with about $47,300. It is ahead of Japan, Kuwait and Poland, and behind Spain, Czechia and Slovenia.

Several places above it are small, such as Monaco, Liechtenstein, Singapore and Luxembourg, where banking or trade can lift the per-person figure. Among larger economies, Israel is in the same band as South Korea, Spain and Japan.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Real GDP per capita, purchasing power parity. Dependencies and the European Union are excluded. Rank is among 196 entries. The West Bank and Gaza share one Factbook figure.
