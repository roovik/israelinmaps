Total fertility rate, 2025 estimates. Israel in blue.

Israel has 2.89 births per woman. The 11 countries of the Group of Ten range from 1.27 in Italy to 1.90 in France. None reaches replacement level. Israel is 0.99 above the highest of them.

Japan, Italy and Canada are among the lowest. A rate far below 2.1 means a shrinking and ageing population without immigration. The Group of Ten has 11 members, and Switzerland is the 11th.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Total fertility rate, 2025 estimate. The Group of Ten is Belgium, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States.
