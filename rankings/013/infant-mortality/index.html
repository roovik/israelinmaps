Infant mortality rate, deaths per 1,000 live births, 2025 estimates. Israel in blue, Iran in brown. Lower is better.

Israel records about 2.6 infant deaths per 1,000 live births. That is the lowest in the group, and about 8 times lower than the 20.9 for the Arab League and Iran together.

Rates of this kind reflect prenatal care, hospitals and public health. They are among the most reliable measures of a country’s health system.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Infant mortality rate, total. The combined bar is the population-weighted average of the 22 Arab League members, counting the West Bank and the Gaza Strip separately, and Iran.
