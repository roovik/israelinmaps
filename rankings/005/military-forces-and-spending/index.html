ISRAEL RANKINGS

Military

Forces and Spending

Israel has about 169,500 active soldiers and 465,000 reservists. Among the Arab League members and Iran, it ranks 7 in active personnel and 2 in defense budget. Its military spending of 8 percent of GDP is the highest share in the group, tied with Algeria.

The table lists the 20 Arab League members that Global Firepower rates, with Iran and Israel. Comoros and the West Bank and Gaza are not rated by Global Firepower. Figures for Syria, Yemen, Libya, Sudan and Somalia reflect states at war or in collapse and are unreliable. Reservist figures are listed as zero where the source shows none.
