Israel against the combined Arab League and Iran, on one scale per row. The percentage is Israel as a share of the rest, and the right-hand figure is how many times larger the rest is.

The full bar is the Arab League and Iran combined, with the Arab League in tan and Iran in brown at the right-hand end. The blue bar is Israel drawn to the same scale. Israel equals 1.7 percent of the population and 0.15 percent of the area, and its economy is about 13 percent of the combined output at exchange rates.

On the military measures Israel’s share is larger. It has 5 percent of the active soldiers, 25 percent of the reserves, 16 percent of the fighter aircraft and 8 percent of the tanks. The military figures cover the 20 League members that Global Firepower rates, and exclude Comoros, the West Bank and Gaza.

Sources. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Population, area, and GDP at exchange rates and at purchasing power parity. Global Firepower 2026 for the military figures. The West Bank and Gaza are one entry in the economic figures.
