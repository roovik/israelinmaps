Median age, total population, 2025 estimates. Israel in blue, Iran in brown.

The median Israeli is 30.2 years old, ranking 9 of 26. The Arab League and Iran average 27.0. Half of Israelis are younger than the median and half are older.

Israel is among the youngest of the developed countries because of its high birth rate, which is shown on the births per woman pages.

Source. CIA World Factbook, last edition before its retirement in February 2026, from the public factbook.json archive. Median age, total population. The combined bar is the population-weighted average of the 22 Arab League members, counting the West Bank and the Gaza Strip separately, and Iran.
