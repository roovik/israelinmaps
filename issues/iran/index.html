Iran

Iran's government calls for Israel's destruction and backs Hamas, Hezbollah and the Houthis. Israel treats Iran's nuclear and missile programs as a threat to its existence. After the twelve-day war of June 2025, the question is what Iran can rebuild and what Israel will do about it.

What is at stake

Nuclear programIsrael and its allies fear that Iran's enrichment of uranium could lead to a bomb. Iran says its program is peaceful.
MissilesIran fired hundreds of missiles and drones at Israel in April and October 2024 and again in June 2025. Most were intercepted, and a few caused deaths and damage.
The alliesHezbollah in Lebanon, the Houthis in Yemen and militias elsewhere are armed and funded by Iran. They are why Israel's wars on several fronts are linked.

How we got here

  1. 1979 Feb

    The Islamic Revolution in Iran. The new government cuts ties with Israel and makes opposition to it a founding principle.

  2. 1981 Jun 7

    Israel destroys the Osirak reactor. Jets strike Iraq’s nuclear reactor near Baghdad before it can go into operation.

  3. 2006 Jul 12 to Aug 14

    The Second Lebanon War. It follows a cross-border Hezbollah attack and ends with a UN ceasefire.

  4. 2015 Mar 3

    Netanyahu addresses Congress on Iran.

  5. 2015 Jul 14

    The Iran nuclear deal. Iran and the world powers sign an agreement that Israel opposes.

  6. 2023 Oct 8

    Hezbollah opens a second front. Hezbollah fires on the north the day after the massacre, and some 60,000 Israelis leave their homes.

  7. 2023 Oct 19

    The Houthis in Yemen launch their first missiles toward Israel.

  8. 2024 Apr 1

    A strike in Damascus kills senior Iranian commanders.

  9. 2024 Apr 13 to 14

    Iran fires missiles and drones at Israel. About 320 missiles and drones are launched.

  10. 2024 Jul 19

    A Houthi drone kills a man in Tel Aviv.

  11. 2024 Jul 27

    A Hezbollah rocket kills 12 children on a football field in Majdal Shams.

  12. 2024 Sep 17 to 18

    Exploding pagers and radios wound thousands of Hezbollah members.

  13. 2024 Sep 27

    Israel kills Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah in Beirut.

  14. 2024 Oct 1

    Iran fires about 180 ballistic missiles at Israel, and ground forces enter southern Lebanon.

  15. 2024 Oct 26

    Israel strikes Iran’s air defenses and missile factories.

  16. 2024 Nov 27

    Ceasefire with Hezbollah. Fighting that began on October 8, 2023 ends.

  17. 2025 Jun 13 to 24

    The twelve-day war with Iran. Israel strikes Iran’s nuclear and missile sites, and the United States joins in.
