Gaza

Israel left Gaza completely in 2005. Hamas took over in 2007 and Gaza has been the center of Israel's wars since then, including the war that followed October 7, 2023. A ceasefire under a United States plan took effect in October 2025, and who controls the Strip is still unresolved.

What is at stake

Who governs GazaHamas says it will not disarm. Israel says it will not accept a Hamas-run Gaza. The peace plan assumes an outside administration and security force, and the details are the hard part.
The hostagesHamas took about 250 hostages on October 7. Most were freed in truces and deals, and the plan's first phase covered the surviving hostages.
RebuildingLarge areas of the Strip are in ruins. Any rebuilding depends on security, funding and who is in charge.

How we got here

  1. 1987 Dec 8

    The First Intifada. A Palestinian uprising begins in Gaza and spreads to Judea and Samaria.

  2. 1992 Dec 17

    Israel deports 415 Hamas activists to Lebanon.

  3. 1994 May 4

    Territory: Israel withdraws from the Gaza Strip and Jericho.

  4. 1994 Oct 9

    Hamas kidnaps soldier Nachshon Wachsman.

  5. 2005 Aug 15 to Sep 12

    Disengagement from Gaza. Israel removes all its soldiers and communities from the Strip.

  6. 2006 Jan

    Hamas wins the Palestinian legislative elections.

  7. 2007 Jun 14

    Hamas takes control of Gaza. Hamas drives out the Palestinian Authority after winning elections in January 2006.

  8. 2008 Dec 27 to 2009 Jan 18

    Operation Cast Lead. A three-week war in Gaza follows years of rocket fire on southern Israel.

  9. 2011 Apr 7

    Iron Dome intercepts its first rocket from Gaza.

  10. 2011 Oct 18

    Gilad Shalit comes home. Hamas frees the soldier after five years in captivity in exchange for 1,027 prisoners.

  11. 2012 Nov 14 to 21

    Operation Pillar of Defense in Gaza.

  12. 2014 Jul 8 to Aug 26

    Operation Protective Edge. A 50-day war with Hamas follows rocket fire and the murder of three Israeli teenagers.

  13. 2019 Nov 12

    Operation Black Belt targets Islamic Jihad in Gaza.

  14. 2021 May 10 to 21

    Operation Guardian of the Walls. An 11-day war with Hamas sets off riots in mixed Arab and Jewish cities.

  15. 2023 Oct 7

    Hamas attacks Israel. About 1,200 people are killed and about 250 are taken hostage.

  16. 2023 Oct 27

    Ground forces enter Gaza.

  17. 2024 Jan 2

    Hamas leader Saleh al-Arouri is killed in Beirut.

  18. 2024 Jul 31

    Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh is killed in Tehran.

  19. 2024 Oct 16

    Israeli soldiers kill Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar in Rafah.

  20. 2025 Oct 10

    Gaza ceasefire and peace plan. A ceasefire takes effect under a United States plan.
