Coalition talks

Israel is governed by coalitions, because no party has ever won a majority of the Knesset's 120 seats on its own. After the 26th Knesset election, no bloc reached 61 seats, so the parties are bargaining over who forms the government and on what terms.

What is at stake

The 61-seat ruleA government needs the support of 61 of 120 Knesset members. The president asks the candidate with the best chance to try to form one, and that candidate has weeks to succeed.
Small parties, big leverageWhen the blocs are close, a party with only a handful of seats can decide who governs. Its demands usually cover ministries, budgets and laws it cares about.
The Haredi partiesShas and United Torah Judaism have often been kingmakers, and the military draft for the ultra-Orthodox is among the hardest issues in any negotiation.

How we got here

  1. 1977 May 17

    The upheaval. Likud wins the May 17 election and Menachem Begin becomes prime minister, ending three decades of Labor rule.

  2. 1983 Oct 10

    Yitzhak Shamir becomes prime minister.

  3. 1984 Jul 23

    Election: Alignment 44 seats, Likud 41.

  4. 1985 Jul 1

    The economic stabilization plan. The unity government brings inflation of over 400 percent down within a year.

  5. 1992 Jul 13

    Rabin returns. Labor wins the June election and Yitzhak Rabin becomes prime minister for the second time.

  6. 1995 Nov 4

    Rabin is assassinated. A Jewish extremist shoots the prime minister after a peace rally in Tel Aviv.

  7. 1996 Jun 18

    Benjamin Netanyahu becomes prime minister after beating Shimon Peres in the May 29 election.

  8. 1999 Jul 6

    Ehud Barak becomes prime minister after beating Netanyahu on May 17.

  9. 2006 Apr 14

    Ehud Olmert becomes prime minister after Kadima wins on March 28.

  10. 2009 Mar 31

    Benjamin Netanyahu returns as prime minister.

  11. 2019 Apr 9

    Election: Likud and Blue and White tie at 35.

  12. 2019 Sep 17

    Election: Blue and White 33 seats, Likud 32.

  13. 2020 Mar 2

    Election: Likud wins 36 seats.

  14. 2020 May 17

    Netanyahu and Benny Gantz form a unity government.

  15. 2021 Jun 13

    Bennett becomes prime minister. A broad coalition ends Netanyahu’s 12 years in office.

  16. 2022 Jul 1

    Yair Lapid becomes prime minister.

  17. 2022 Dec 29

    Benjamin Netanyahu returns as prime minister after the November 1 election.

  18. 2026 Oct 27

    Election: scheduled.

  19. 2026 Oct 27

    Election for the 26th Knesset. No bloc reaches 61 seats, and coalition talks begin.
