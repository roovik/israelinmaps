Haredi draft

Most Jewish Israelis, men and women, serve in the army. Many ultra-Orthodox (Haredi) men have instead studied in yeshivas under a deferment that began in 1948. The Supreme Court has repeatedly ruled that the exemption needs a legal basis, and the war has made the question more urgent and more divisive.

What is at stake

The argument for exemptionHaredi leaders say that full-time Torah study is a national and religious mission, that army life endangers their communities' way of life, and that their numbers should not be forced into uniform.
The argument for a draftCritics, including many soldiers and reservists, say that service should be shared by everyone, especially during the war, and that a deferment for one large group is unfair.
The coalition linkThe Haredi parties usually insist on protecting the exemption as a condition for joining a coalition, which ties the draft to every round of coalition talks.

How we got here

  1. 1948

    David Ben-Gurion exempts about 400 yeshiva students from service so that Torah study can continue after the Holocaust.

  2. 1977

    Menachem Begin’s government, which includes Haredi parties, widens the deferment, and the number of exempt students grows.

  3. 1999

    The Supreme Court rules that the defense minister has no authority to grant the exemption on his own, which pushes the Knesset to legislate.

  4. 2002

    The Knesset passes the Tal Law, which allows yeshiva students to defer service and later be exempted.

  5. 2012

    The Supreme Court strikes down the Tal Law as unequal. The law expires and the argument begins again.

  6. 2014

    The Knesset passes a new law with draft targets and penalties for the Haredi community. The Supreme Court strikes down its main parts in 2017.

  7. 2023 Oct 7

    The war creates a large need for troops. The question of the exemption becomes more urgent.

  8. 2024 Jun 25

    The Supreme Court unanimously rules that the government must begin drafting yeshiva students.

  9. 2025 Jul

    United Torah Judaism and Shas leave the government in a dispute over a draft bill. Shas stays in the coalition for a time as a supporter.

  10. 2026 Oct 27

    The 26th Knesset election puts the Haredi parties at the center of the coalition talks.
