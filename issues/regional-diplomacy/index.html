Regional diplomacy

Israel now has peace treaties with Egypt and Jordan, and normalized ties with the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Morocco and Sudan under the Abraham Accords. The region is still being reordered, with Turkey pushing into Syria and Lebanon, Gulf states weighing their next steps and Saudi Arabia still undecided.

What is at stake

Treaties that heldThe peace with Egypt (1979) and Jordan (1994) has lasted through wars and crises, though both are often called a cold peace.
The Abraham AccordsThe UAE and Bahrain normalized ties in 2020, followed by Morocco and Sudan. Trade, flights and tourism grew quickly.
The next stepsSaudi Arabia has linked normalization to progress toward a Palestinian state. Turkey's role in Syria and Lebanon, and the new government in Damascus, change Israel's northern calculations.

How we got here

  1. 1967 Nov 22

    Resolution 242. The UN Security Council calls for Israeli withdrawal from territories and for peace and secure borders.

  2. 1977 Nov 19 to 21

    Sadat comes to Jerusalem. The Egyptian president addresses the Knesset and opens the way to peace.

  3. 1978 Sep 5 to 17

    Summit: Begin and Sadat meet with President Carter at Camp David.

  4. 1979 Mar 26

    Peace with Egypt. The Camp David summit of September 1978 leads to the first peace treaty with an Arab state.

  5. 1981 Oct 6

    Anwar Sadat is assassinated in Cairo.

  6. 1982 Apr 25

    Israel leaves Sinai. The last of the peninsula returns to Egypt under the peace treaty, and the town of Yamit is evacuated.

  7. 1991 Oct 30

    The Madrid Conference. Israel and its Arab neighbors open their first direct peace talks.

  8. 1993 Sep 13

    The Oslo Accords. Israel and the PLO sign the Declaration of Principles on the White House lawn.

  9. 1994 Oct 26

    Peace with Jordan. Rabin and King Hussein sign the treaty and normalize relations.

  10. 2000 Sep 28

    The Second Intifada. After the Camp David summit fails, a wave of suicide bombings and fighting lasts until 2005.

  11. 2016

    UN Security Council Resolution 2334 criticizes Israeli communities in Judea and Samaria.

  12. 2018 May 14

    The United States moves its embassy to Jerusalem. It follows the 2017 recognition of Jerusalem as Israel’s capital.

  13. 2020 Sep 15

    The Abraham Accords. The United Arab Emirates and Bahrain sign at the White House and normalize relations with Israel.
