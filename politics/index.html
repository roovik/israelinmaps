How a government is formed

1. The President choosesAfter consulting the parties, the President asks the member with the best chance of a majority to try. That is usually a party leader.
2. 61 of 120The candidate has 28 days to sign up 61 members, and the President can extend that by up to 14 days.
3. If it failsThe President can turn to another member. If no one succeeds, the Knesset itself can choose a candidate, and if that fails too, the country votes again.
