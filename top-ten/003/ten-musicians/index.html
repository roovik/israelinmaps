Ten Musicians

Ten musicians who shaped the sound of the country, in the order of their place on the timelines. Not all were born in Israel.

1 Bronislaw Hubermanברוניסלב הוברמן1882 TO 1947 The Polish violinist who founded the Palestine Symphony Orchestra in 1936 and gave Jewish musicians fleeing Europe a new home.

TIMELINE 10

2 Arturo Toscaniniארתורו טוסקניני1867 TO 1957 The Italian conductor who led the orchestra’s first concert in 1936, as a stand against fascism.

TIMELINE 10

3 Itzhak Perlmanיצחק פרלמןBORN 1945 Born in Tel Aviv, he had polio as a child and became one of the best-known violinists in the world.

TIMELINE 11

4 Naomi Shemerנעמי שמר1930 TO 2004 She wrote Jerusalem of Gold in 1967, a song that became a second anthem after the Six-Day War.

TIMELINES 10,

5 Zubin Mehtaזובין מהטהBORN 1936 Music director of the Israel Philharmonic from 1977 and music director for life from 1981.

TIMELINE 14

6 Ofra Hazaעפרה חזה1957 TO 2000 A singer from a Yemenite Jewish family in Tel Aviv. She placed second at Eurovision in 1983 and reached audiences around the world.

TIMELINE 15

7 Dana Internationalדנה אינטרנשיונלBORN 1972 She won the Eurovision Song Contest in 1998 with Diva and was the first openly transgender winner.

TIMELINE 16

8 Daniel Barenboimדניאל ברנבויםBORN 1942 Pianist and conductor who founded the West-Eastern Divan Orchestra with Edward Said in 1999.

TIMELINE 16

9 Arik Einsteinאריק איינשטיין1939 TO 2013 A leading voice of Israeli pop and rock from the 1960s onward. Crowds gathered in Tel Aviv to mourn him.

TIMELINE 18

10 Netta Barzilaiנטע ברזיליBORN 1993

She won Eurovision in 2018 with Toy, and Tel Aviv hosted the contest in 2019.
