Ten Painters

Ten painters who gave the land its images, in the order of their place on the timelines. Some also made sculpture.

1 David Roberts 1796 TO 1864 The Scottish painter toured the land in 1839 and 1840. His lithographs made it familiar to readers in Europe.

TIMELINE 5

2 Nahum Gutmanנחום גוטמן1898 TO 1980 Painter, author and illustrator who grew up in Tel Aviv. His mosaics and murals decorate the city.

TIMELINE 7

3 Anna Tichoאנה טיכו1894 TO 1980 She drew the hills of Jerusalem for decades. Her house is now part of the Israel Museum.

TIMELINE 8

4 Abel Pannאבל פן1883 TO 1963 He drew Jewish suffering in the First World War and painted scenes from the Bible. He worked at the Bezalel school.

TIMELINE 8

5 Reuven Rubinראובן רובין1893 TO 1974 His bright landscapes helped create a local style of painting. He was Israel’s first envoy to Romania.

TIMELINE 9

6 Mordecai Ardonמרדכי ארדון1896 TO 1992 A former Bauhaus student who directed the Bezalel school from 1940 to 1952. He later made stained glass windows for the National Library.

TIMELINE 10

7 Marcel Jancoמרסל ינקו1895 TO 1984 A founder of Dada in Zurich. He came to the land in 1941 and helped start the artists’ village of Ein Hod.

TIMELINE 11

8 Marc Chagallמארק שאגאל1887 TO 1985 His stained glass windows for the Hadassah synagogue in Jerusalem were dedicated in 1962.

TIMELINE 13

9 Yaacov Agamיעקב אגםBORN 1928 A pioneer of kinetic art, born in Rishon LeZion. His Fire and Water Fountain stands in Dizengoff Square in Tel Aviv.

TIMELINE 15

10 Menashe Kadishmanמנשה קדישמן1932 TO 2015

He painted sheep for decades and made Fallen Leaves, a floor of iron faces, for the Jewish Museum in Berlin.
