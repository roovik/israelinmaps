Ten Authors

Ten writers and poets who gave modern Hebrew its voice, in the order of their place on the timelines.

1 Hayim Nahman Bialikחיים נחמן ביאליק1873 TO 1934 The Hebrew national poet. He settled in Tel Aviv in 1924, and his house there is now a museum.

TIMELINES 9, 10

2 Leah Goldbergלאה גולדברג1911 TO 1970 Poet and children’s author who settled in Tel Aviv in 1935 and later taught at the Hebrew University.

TIMELINE 10

3 Natan Altermanנתן אלתרמן1910 TO 1970 Poet whose Silver Platter, about the cost of statehood, became a classic of the War of Independence years.

TIMELINES 9, 11

4 Yehuda Amichaiיהודה עמיחי1924 TO 2000 The poet of Jerusalem. His first collection appeared in 1955, and he won the Israel Prize in 1982.

TIMELINES 10,

5 Ephraim Kishonאפרים קישון1924 TO 2005 Satirist and director whose film Sallah Shabati was the first Israeli film nominated for an Academy Award.

TIMELINE 13

6 S. Y. Agnonש״י עגנון1888 TO 1970 Hebrew novelist who lived in Jerusalem and won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1966.

TIMELINES 7, 13

7 A. B. Yehoshuaא״ב יהושע1936 TO 2022 Novelist whose books include Mr. Mani, published in 1990. He taught at the University of Haifa.

TIMELINES 10,

8 Etgar Keretאתגר קרתBORN 1967 Writer of short stories and film scripts. His first collection, Pipelines, appeared in 1992.

TIMELINE 16

9 Amos Ozעמוס עוז1939 TO 2018 Novelist whose memoir A Tale of Love and Darkness of 2002 became his best-known book. He argued for two states.

TIMELINES 10,

10 David Grossmanדויד גרוסמןBORN 1954 Novelist of To the End of the Land. He won the Man Booker International Prize in 2017.
