Ten Nobel Prize Winners

Ten laureates tied to Israel, in date order. Yasser Arafat shared the 1994 Peace Prize with Yitzhak Rabin and Shimon Peres.

1 S. Y. Agnonש״י עגנוןLITERATURE, 1966 Shared the prize with the German poet Nelly Sachs. He was the first Israeli to win a Nobel Prize.

5 Daniel Kahnemanדניאל כהנמןECONOMICS, 2002

Born in Tel Aviv and trained at the Hebrew University. He shared the prize with Vernon Smith for work on how people decide under uncertainty.

TIMELINE 17

6 Aaron Ciechanoverאהרן צ׳חנוברCHEMISTRY, 2004 Shared the prize with Avram Hershko and Irwin Rose for discovering how cells mark proteins for destruction.

TIMELINE 17

7 Avram Hershkoאברהם הרשקוCHEMISTRY, 2004 Shared the prize with Aaron Ciechanover and Irwin Rose. Both worked at the Technion in Haifa.

TIMELINE 17

8 Robert Aumannישראל אומןECONOMICS, 2005 A professor at the Hebrew University who shared the prize with Thomas Schelling for work on game theory and conflict.

TIMELINE 17

9 Ada Yonathעדה יונתCHEMISTRY, 2009 Mapped the structure of the ribosome. She is the first Israeli woman to win a Nobel Prize in science.

TIMELINE 17

10 Dan Shechtmanדן שכטמןCHEMISTRY, 2011 A Technion scientist who won for the discovery of quasicrystals.
