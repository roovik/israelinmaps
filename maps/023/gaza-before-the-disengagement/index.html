HISTORICAL MAPS

MAP 23 Gaza before the Disengagement

Gush Katif and the Strip, August 2005

In August 2005, Israel withdrew civilians and military bases from the Gaza Strip, removing 21 Jewish communities from the enclave of Gush Katif and northern Gaza, and expelling about 7,000 citizens, many of them protesting non- violently.

The Gaza Disengagement was part of Israel’s unilateral plan to disengage from the Strip. Implemented under Prime Minister Ariel Sharon, the move aimed to reduce friction between Israelis and Arabs without waiting for a negotiated agreement. The 21 Jewish communities, home to thriving agricultural settlements, were dismantled, and their residents were removed by the IDF.

The Disengagement exposed the deep emotional and ideological divide within Israeli society. Despite hopes that it would reduce violence, Gaza fell under Hamas control in 2006, leading to frequent rocket attacks and military escalations, setting the stage for October 7, 2023 and what followed.

TIMELINE

Israel takes the Gaza Strip from Egypt in the Six-Day War.
