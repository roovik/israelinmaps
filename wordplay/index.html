Hebrew Wordplay

One Hebrew word, several meanings. Each entry follows a word through the places it turns up, from the Bible to the street. Where scholars disagree about a link, the entry says so.

HHebrew WordplayHoduהודוHebrew for "give thanks," and also the word for India and for the turkey.Hebrew WordplayShalomשלוםHello, goodbye and peace, from a root that means whole.Hebrew WordplayChaiחיAlive, life and the number 18.Hebrew WordplayLechemלחםBread, and perhaps the same letters as fight and war.Hebrew WordplaySeferספרBook, number, story, scribe and barber, all from one root.Hebrew WordplayKesefכסףSilver, money and longing share one root.SHebrew WordplayShemeshשמשThe sun, and also the helper candle on a Hanukkah menorah.Hebrew WordplayMazalמזלLuck, and the word for a star sign.Hebrew WordplayBalaganבלגןA mess, from a Russian word for a market booth.Hebrew WordplayDavkaדווקאSpecifically, of all things, on purpose, out of spite.Hebrew WordplayTo playלשחקIn Hebrew the verb to play is a near neighbor of the verb to laugh.Hebrew WordplayGzrגזרCut out and decree share one root. The carrot spells the same but is a borrowed word.Hebrew WordplayChalalחללOuter space, to desecrate and a flute, from a root about hollows and openings.Hebrew WordplayAdamאדםMan, earth and red, the three words Genesis plays on.Hebrew WordplayShevaשבעSeven, to swear and sated, with one famous well in the middle.Hebrew WordplayBerechברךKnee, to bless and a pool, a family whose links scholars still debate.Hebrew WordplayRegelרגלFoot, festival and habit, three words walking the same road.Hebrew WordplayKerenקרןHorn, ray and fund, from a root that sticks out and holds things.Hebrew WordplayChashavחשבTo think, to count and important, from one verb of the mind.Hebrew WordplayAvadעבדTo work, a slave and a fact, from the verb that defines labor.Hebrew WordplayAmanאמןBelief, craft and amen, from a root meaning firm.Hebrew WordplayChayilחילArmy, valor and wealth, the word behind a woman of valor.
