Hebrew Wordplay

One Hebrew word, several meanings. Each entry follows a word through the places it turns up, from the Bible to the street. Where scholars disagree about a link, the entry says so.

HHebrew WordplayHoduהודוHebrew for "give thanks," and also the word for India and for the turkey.SHebrew WordplayShalomשלוםHello, goodbye and peace, from a root that means whole.CHebrew WordplayChaiחיAlive, life and the number 18.LHebrew WordplayLechemלחםBread, and perhaps the same letters as fight and war.SHebrew WordplaySeferספרBook, number, story, scribe and barber, all from one root.KHebrew WordplayKesefכסףSilver, money and longing share one root.SHebrew WordplayShemeshשמשThe sun, and also the helper candle on a Hanukkah menorah.MHebrew WordplayMazalמזלLuck, and the word for a star sign.BHebrew WordplayBalaganבלגןA mess, from a Russian word for a market booth.DHebrew WordplayDavkaדווקאSpecifically, of all things, on purpose, out of spite.THebrew WordplayTo playלשחקIn Hebrew the verb to play is a near neighbor of the verb to laugh.
