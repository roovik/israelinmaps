Sources

Each source has its own page here with its latest news and views.

  • The Times of IsraelAn Israeli English-language news site covering Israel, the Middle East and the Jewish world, with a large opinion and blogs section.
  • The Jerusalem PostAn Israeli English-language daily with reporting on Israel, the region and Jewish communities, plus opinion and analysis.
  • YnetnewsThe English edition of Ynet, the news site of the Yedioth Ahronoth group.
  • HaaretzThe English edition of the Israeli daily Haaretz, with news, analysis and opinion.
  • JNSJewish News Syndicate, a news service on Israel, the Middle East and Jewish communities worldwide.
  • Israel InsiderAn Israeli news and commentary site, now published through NOWlej.
  • Israel HayomThe English edition of the Israeli daily Israel Hayom.
