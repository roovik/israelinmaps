Special pricing for organizations. Schools, congregations, youth groups, tour programs and other organizations can order ten copies or more at a group price, and can license the PDF Interactive Edition of any title for classes and members. Ask us below.

PDF Interactive Edition. Every title also comes as a Digital Edition: a PDF that behaves like a small website. Roll over a name, place or term and a short explanation appears. Click it and you jump to its glossary entry, map, timeline or picture. Israel NOWlej alone holds more than 7,500 cross-links and about 2,000 rollover notes in its 243 pages.

Each page also carries a link and a QR code to the matching page online, so the PDF stays tied to the live maps, timelines and news. It prints cleanly for handouts and projects well in a classroom. The rollover notes need the free Adobe Acrobat Reader on a computer; the links work in any PDF viewer.

The Digital Edition is licensed to each school or organization and marked with its name. It is not a public download. Ask for the PDF edition.

Made to teach

The books and the site are educational media, built to be looked at first and read second. A map, timeline or chart leads every spread, and a short text explains it.

  • Visual. Maps, timelines, charts, rankings and tours that show four thousand years of history and the modern state at a glance.
  • Connected. Every page of the books carries a QR code that opens the matching page on the site, so a class can go from the printed page to the screen.
  • Ready for a room. The interactive PDF editions and the site work on a projector, a smartboard or a phone.
  • Easy to look things up. An encyclopedic illustrated glossary of more than 500 entries, in print and online.
