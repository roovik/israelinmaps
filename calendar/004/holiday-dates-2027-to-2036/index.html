ISRAEL IN TIME CALENDARS

Reference Holiday Dates, 2027 to 2036

When each holiday and day of memory falls, year by year.

Holiday dates for each Gregorian year from 2027 to 2036. Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot and Hanukkah fall in autumn and winter, the other days in spring and summer. Hanukkah is shown for its first full day, and it lasts eight days. The days of memory follow the Israeli rules for moving them away from the Sabbath: Yom HaShoah moves to Thursday if it falls on Friday and to Monday if it falls on Sunday, Independence Day moves earlier from Friday or Saturday to Thursday and later from Monday to Tuesday with Yom HaZikaron the day before it, and Tisha B’Av moves to Sunday if it falls on the Sabbath.
