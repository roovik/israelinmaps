How peace came in waves

1979: EgyptEgypt was the first Arab state to make peace, after the Camp David Accords. Israel left the Sinai in stages.
1994: JordanJordan signed a treaty with Israel a year after the Oslo Accords, giving Israel a second peace across a long border.
2020: Abraham AccordsThe United Arab Emirates and Bahrain normalized ties with Israel, followed by Morocco and Sudan. The United States brokered them.
