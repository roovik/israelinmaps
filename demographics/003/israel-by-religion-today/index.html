Within the Arab group: Muslims 1,849,000, Arab Christians about 145,000, Druze about 153,000. All Christians, Arab and non-Arab, number 184,200.

CBS reports two overlapping systems. By religion, Jews are 73.6 percent of residents and “Others” 4.5 percent. By its headline category, “Jews and Others” are 76.3 percent and “Arabs” 21.1 percent. Arabs are Muslims, Druze and the roughly four in five Christians who are Arab.

Sources. CBS table for the end of 2024, total 9,805,100, as reproduced by Wikipedia. “Others” is the residual after Jews, Muslims, Christians and Druze. CBS headline totals for the end of 2025 from the CBS release reported by The Times of Israel and the Jerusalem Post. Muslims 1.849 million from CBS as reported by JNS. Christians 184,200 from CBS as reported in January 2026. The 2025 headline groups, “Jews and Others” and “Arabs”, are the CBS categories, and the 2025 Arab total includes about 145,000 Arab Christians and about 153,000 Druze.
