Sources. Areas are total areas, including inland water. CIA World Factbook, Geography, Area, total, for each country. The Factbook was retired by the CIA on 4 February 2026, so the figures are the last published edition, read from the public factbook.json archive of its data. Israel is the Factbook entry for Israel. The Arab League total sums the 22 members, with the West Bank and the Gaza Strip counted together as the League does. Judea and Samaria is the Factbook entry for the territory, listed there under another name, and its figure includes the Latrun Salient and the northwest quarter of the Dead Sea. The France figure is metropolitan France only. The Spain figure includes the Balearic and Canary Islands. US states: US Census Bureau, State Area Measurements and Internal Point Coordinates, 2010. England: Wikipedia infobox, to be checked against the ONS Standard Area Measurements before print. Outlines on the maps: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 (public domain), and for US states the us-states GeoJSON in the PublicaMundi Mapping I repository, from US Census boundary data. The maps use simplified outlines, so areas measured from them differ slightly from these official totals. Square miles are km² divided by 2.59.
