REFERENCE TABLE 4 · SUMMITS AND TERRITORY

Reference table 4 of 4

Summits and territorial changes

Agreements, summits and changes of control, in date order.

DATE EVENT CHANGE

1947 Nov 29 UN partition plan The General Assembly votes for a Jewish state and an Arab state.

1949 Feb 24 to Jul 20 Armistice agreements The Green Line is drawn. Israel holds more land than the partition plan gave it.

1950 Apr 24 Jordan annexes Judea and Samaria East Jerusalem is included.

1957 Mar Sinai withdrawal Israel leaves Sinai and Gaza after the Sinai War.

1967 Jun 7 Jerusalem reunited Israel takes the Old City. Israeli law is extended to East Jerusalem on June 28.

1967 Aug 29 to Sep 1 Khartoum summit Arab leaders refuse peace, recognition and negotiation with Israel.

1967 Nov 22 Resolution 242 The UN Security Council calls for withdrawal and secure borders.

1974 Jan 18 Sinai I agreement Israel and Egypt separate their forces.

1974 May 31 Golan disengagement Israel and Syria separate their forces.

1975 Sep 4 Sinai II agreement Israel returns the Abu Rudeis oil fields and the passes.

1977 Nov 19 to 21 Sadat in Jerusalem Anwar Sadat addresses the Knesset.

1978 Sep 5 to 17 Camp David summit Begin and Sadat agree on a framework with President Carter.

1979 Mar 26 Israel and Egypt sign a peace treaty Israel returns Sinai in stages and completes it on Apr 25, 1982.

1980 Jul 30 Jerusalem Law The Knesset declares the united city the capital.

1981 Dec 14 Golan Heights Law Israeli law is applied to the Golan Heights.

1988 Jul 31 Jordan drops its claim King Hussein cuts legal ties with Judea and Samaria.

1989 Mar 15 Taba returned Israel gives Taba to Egypt after arbitration.

1991 Oct 30 Madrid Conference The first direct Arab and Israeli talks.

1993 Sep 13 Oslo Accords Israel and the PLO sign the Declaration of Principles in Washington.

1994 May 4 Gaza-Jericho agreement Israel withdraws from the Gaza Strip and Jericho.

1994 Oct 26 Israel and Jordan sign a peace treaty The border is set.

1995 Sep 28 Oslo II Judea and Samaria is divided into Areas A, B and C.

1997 Jan 15 Hebron Protocol Israel withdraws from most of Hebron.

1998 Oct 23 Wye River Memorandum Netanyahu and Arafat agree on further withdrawals.

2000 Jul 11 to 25 Camp David summit Barak and Arafat fail to reach agreement.

2000 May 24 Lebanon withdrawal Israel leaves southern Lebanon.

2005 Feb 8 Sharm el-Sheikh summit Sharon and Abbas announce an end to violence.

2005 Aug 15 to Sep 12 Gaza disengagement Israel removes its soldiers and communities from Gaza.

2007 Nov 27 Annapolis Conference Talks are relaunched.

2019 Mar 25 Golan recognition The United States recognizes Israeli sovereignty.

2020 Sep 15 Abraham Accords The UAE and Bahrain normalize relations. Morocco and Sudan follow.

2024 Dec Syria After the fall of the Assad regime Israel moves into the buffer zone on the Golan.
