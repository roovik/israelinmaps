TURNING POINTS ALSO IN THIS DECADE

Mordecai Ardon reaches

Jerusalem. A former Bauhaus student, he directs the Bezalel school from 1940.

1933 to 1936 The Fifth Aliyah. More than

170,000 Jews flee Nazi Germany and Eastern Europe. Many are doctors,

lawyers, musicians and

architects.

1936 Apr 19 to 1939 The Arab Revolt. It protests

Jewish immigration and British policy and lasts until

1939.

1938 Oct 2 The Tiberias massacre. Arab

gunmen kill 19 Jews, 11 of them children, in the heart of

the Arab Revolt.

1939 May 17 The White Paper. Britain

sharply limits Jewish immigration and land purchases as the Holocaust

begins.

1939 to 1945 The Holocaust. Six million

Jews are murdered in Europe. Survivors seek refuge

in the land.

1930 Jul 13 Naomi Shemer is born at Kibbutz Kinneret.

1930 Oct The Passfield White Paper curbs Jewish immigration. 1931 The Irgun, an armed underground group, is founded.

1931 The Habima theater makes its home in Tel Aviv. 1931 The census counts about 175,000 Jews. 1931 Apr 16 The poet Rachel dies in Tel Aviv.

1932 Mar The first Maccabiah games open in Tel Aviv.

1933 The Fifth Aliyah begins as the Nazis take power in Germany.

1933 Jun 16 Haim Arlosoroff is assassinated on a Tel Aviv beach. 1933 Aug 25 The Haavara agreement lets German Jews move some assets.

1934 Aliyah Bet, illegal immigration by sea, begins.

1934 Jul 4 Hayim Nahman Bialik dies in Vienna.

1930s Architects trained at the Bauhaus build the White City of Tel Aviv. 1935 The poet Leah Goldberg settles in Tel Aviv. 1935 David Ben-Gurion becomes chairman of the Jewish Agency.

1935 Yehuda Amichai arrives from Germany as a child. 1935 Sep 15 The Nuremberg Laws strip German Jews of citizenship.

1936 The Palestine Symphony Orchestra, founded by Bronislaw Huberman, gives its first concert, conducted by Arturo Toscanini. 1936 Mar 30 The Palestine Broadcasting Service begins.

1936 Mar The Tel Aviv port opens after riots close Jaffa. 1936 Dec 10 The tower and stockade settlements begin at Tel Amal. 1936 Dec 9 A. B. Yehoshua is born in Jerusalem.

1937 Jul 7 The Peel Commission proposes partition. The Arab leaders reject it. 1937 Aug The Zionist Congress accepts the principle of partition.

1938 Jun Orde Wingate forms the Special Night Squads. 1938 Jul 6 to 15 The Evian Conference fails to open doors to Jewish refugees.

1938 Nov 9 to 10 Kristallnacht burns synagogues across Germany. 1938 Nov The Woodhead Commission shelves partition. 1939 Feb 7 to Mar 17 Summit: the London Conference fails to find agreement.

1939 May 4 Amos Oz is born in Jerusalem. 1939 Sep 1 The Second World War begins.
