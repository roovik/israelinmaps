The Seven-Front War

October 8, 2023 to today

The day after the massacre, Israel is at war on seven fronts. Hezbollah’s leaders fall, Assad’s regime collapses and the living hostages come home. In 2026 war with Iran returns, Hezbollah reopens the north, and Israel and Lebanon hold their first direct talks in decades.

TURNING POINTS

2023 Oct 8 Hezbollah opens a second front. Hezbollah fires on the north the day after the massacre, and some 60,000 Israelis leave their homes.

2024 Nov 27 Ceasefire with Hezbollah. Fighting that began on October 8, 2023 ends.

2024 Dec 8 The Assad regime falls in Syria. Israel moves to secure its Golan border.

2025 Jun 13 to 24 The twelve-day war with Iran. Israel strikes Iran’s nuclear and missile sites, and the United States joins in.

2025 Oct 10 Gaza ceasefire and peace plan. A ceasefire takes effect under a United States plan. On October 13 the 20 living hostages come home and Trump addresses the Knesset.

2026 Jan 26 The last hostage comes home. The body of police officer Ran Gvili is recovered from Gaza. No Israeli hostages remain.

2026 Feb 28 War with Iran. Israel and the United States strike Iran and kill Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iranian missiles hit Israeli cities for weeks.

2026 Apr 14 Direct talks with Lebanon. After a ground war against Hezbollah, Israel and Lebanon meet in Washington, their first direct negotiations since 1993.

ALSO IN THIS PERIOD

2023 Oct 19 The Houthis in Yemen launch their first missiles toward Israel.

2023 Oct 27 Ground forces enter Gaza.

2023 Nov 24 to Dec 1 A truce frees 105 hostages in exchange for Palestinian prisoners.

2024 Jan 2 Hamas leader Saleh al-Arouri is killed in Beirut.

2024 Apr 13 to 14 Iran fires some 320 missiles and drones at Israel. Nearly all are intercepted.

2024 May 6 Israeli forces move into Rafah.

2024 Jun 8 Operation Arnon rescues four hostages from Nuseirat.

2024 Jul 27 A Hezbollah rocket kills 12 children on a football field in Majdal Shams.

2024 Jul 31 Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh is killed in Tehran.

2024 Sep 17 to 18 Exploding pagers and radios wound thousands of Hezbollah members.

2024 Sep 27 Israel kills Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah in Beirut.

2024 Oct 1 Iran fires about 180 ballistic missiles at Israel, and ground forces enter southern Lebanon.

2024 Oct 16 Israeli soldiers kill Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar in Rafah.

2024 Nov 21 The International Criminal Court issues arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant.

2025 Jan 19 A Gaza ceasefire begins. Over six weeks 25 living hostages come home.

2025 Feb 20 Hamas returns the bodies of Shiri Bibas and her sons Ariel and Kfir.

2025 Mar 18 Israel resumes the war in Gaza.

2025 May 4 A Houthi missile lands beside Ben Gurion Airport.

2025 May 21 Two Israeli embassy staff are murdered in Washington.

2025 Jul 16 Israel strikes Damascus to protect the Druze of Sweida.

2025 Aug 28 Israel kills the Houthi prime minister and much of his cabinet in Sanaa.

2025 Sep 9 Israel strikes Hamas leaders in Doha, Qatar.

2025 Sep 21 to 22 Britain, France, Canada, Australia and others recognize a Palestinian state.

2025 Nov 17 The UN Security Council backs the peace plan, a Board of Peace and a stabilization force for Gaza.

2025 Nov 23 Israel kills Hezbollah military chief Haytham Ali Tabatabai in Beirut.

2025 Dec 14 Terrorists murder 15 people at a Hanukkah celebration on Bondi Beach in Sydney.

2025 Dec 26 Israel becomes the first country to recognize Somaliland.

2026 Jan 22 The Board of Peace is launched at Davos. Phase two of the Gaza plan begins.

2026 Feb 2 The Rafah crossing between Gaza and Egypt reopens.

2026 Mar 1 An Iranian missile kills eight in Beit Shemesh.

2026 Mar 3 Hezbollah joins the war, and ground forces return to southern Lebanon.

2026 Mar 17 Israel kills Ali Larijani, head of Iran’s security council.

2026 Mar 28 The Houthis fire missiles at Israel again.

2026 Apr 5 An Iranian missile kills four in a Haifa apartment building.

2026 Apr 8 A United States and Iran ceasefire halts the missile war.

2026 Apr 17 A truce with Lebanon takes effect and is extended.

2026 May 15 Israel kills Izz al-Din al-Haddad, the Hamas leader in Gaza.

2026 May 31 Israeli forces cross the Litani and take Beaufort Castle.

2026 Jun 17 The United States and Iran sign a memorandum to end the war.

2026 Jun 26 Israel and Lebanon sign a framework in Washington for Israeli withdrawal and Hezbollah’s disarmament. Hezbollah rejects it.

2026 Jul 20 Israel hands its first buffer zone sections in southern Lebanon to the Lebanese army.

2026 Sep 30 A Flydubai flight to Tel Aviv diverts to Saudi Arabia. Israel says the co-pilot attacked the pilot.

2026 Oct 27 The Knesset election is set for this day.
