Moshe Dayan משה דיין

People and Groups

Israeli general and politician, 1915 to 1981, who lost an eye fighting in Lebanon in 1941. He was chief of staff during the Suez Crisis, defense minister in the Six-Day War and foreign minister under Menachem Begin.
