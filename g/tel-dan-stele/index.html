Tel Dan Stele כתובת תל דן

Works

Fragment of a victory inscription found at Tel Dan in 1993 and dated to the 9th century BCE. It mentions the "House of David," the earliest known reference to David's dynasty outside the Bible. It is in the Israel Museum.
