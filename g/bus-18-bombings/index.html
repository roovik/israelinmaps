Bus 18 bombings 18 פיגועי קו

Events

Two suicide bombings of the No. 18 bus in Jerusalem on 25 February and 3 March 1996 that killed 45 people. Hamas was behind them, and they shook public support for the Oslo Accords before the 1996 election.
