Nahum Gutman נחום גוטמן

People and Groups

Painter, sculptor and author, 1898 to 1980, who came to the land with his family in 1905 and grew up in the new city of Tel Aviv. He illustrated children's books and made mosaics and murals that decorate Tel Aviv.
