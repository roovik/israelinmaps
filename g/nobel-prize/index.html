Nobel Prize פרס נובל

Concepts

International prizes awarded since 1901. Israelis have won in chemistry, literature, economics and peace, among them Ada Yonath, S.Y. Agnon, Menachem Begin, Yitzhak Rabin, Shimon Peresand Daniel Kahneman.
