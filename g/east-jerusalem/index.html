East Jerusalem מזרח ירושלים

Places

Part of Jerusalem east of the 1949 armistice line, including the Old City. Jordan held it from 1948 to 1967 and Israel took it in the Six-Day War. Israel applied its law there in 1980, which most countries do not recognize.
