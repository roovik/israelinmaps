Merneptah Stele מצבת מרנפתח

Works

Victory monument of the Egyptian pharaoh Merneptah, about 1208 BCE. A line on it names Israel, the earliest known mention of Israel outside the Bible. It stands in the Egyptian Museum in Cairo.
