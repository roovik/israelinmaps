Chalal חלל

Hebrew Wordplay

Chalal is the modern Hebrew word for outer space, and the same three letters give lechalel, to desecrate, and chalil, a flute. Strong's concordance glosses the flute as "perforated," a pipe bored through. Dictionaries tie the three together through the idea of a hollow or an opening: a void, a pierced reed, a thing opened up. The same root also gives lehachel, to begin, as if by an opening wedge. Scholars explain the links in different ways, so read the connection as likely, not settled.Continue ▸
