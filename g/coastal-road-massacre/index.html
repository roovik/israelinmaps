Coastal Road massacre טבח כביש החוף

Events

Attack of 11 March 1978 in which Fatah gunmen landed from the sea, hijacked a bus on the coastal highway and killed 38 civilians, 13 of them children. Israel invaded southern Lebanon three days later.
